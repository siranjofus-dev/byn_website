Legal Compliance

Data Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019) & ODPC Regulations

1. Legal Entity & Data Controller Information

This website (buildyourname.africa) and the BYN SFA platform are operated by Build Your Name Ltd ("#BYN", "we", "us", or "our"), a registered limited liability company headquartered at 10 Nanyuki Rd, Industrial Area, Nairobi, Kenya. Build Your Name Ltd is registered as a Data Controller with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal and operational data necessary to deliver our services:

  • Contact and Scoping Data: When you complete an enquiry form or request a PageSpeed audit, we collect your name, phone number, email address, website domain, and project details.
  • BYN SFA Demo Information: Distribution company name, SKU categories, number of field sales representatives, and van fleet numbers.
  • Technical & Analytics Logs: Anonymized IP addresses, device types, browser versions, and interaction events (such as clicks on WhatsApp, case studies, or price toggles) strictly for performance optimization and debugging.

3. Cookie Usage & Consent Mechanism

Unlike legacy websites that inject third-party marketing tags without consent, our website strictly enforces a privacy-first consent gate:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for site navigation, security token validation (Turnstile), and remembering your session. These cannot be disabled.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Used to monitor Core Web Vitals (LCP, TTFB, INP) and page speed. These are only activated after you grant explicit consent via our cookie banner.

4. Security of Data & Endpoint Protection

In accordance with our technical rebuild standards, all data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3/HSTS). Our backend restricts all WordPress REST user endpoints to prevent username enumeration, and daily automated off-site backups are encrypted at rest with AES-256.

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, you have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request the erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Withdraw your consent to marketing communications at any time.
