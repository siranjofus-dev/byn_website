Distribution Capabilities

Four Interlocked Distribution Modules

Everything your drivers, field merchandisers, and sales directors need on one synchronized platform.

Module 1 · Logistics

Smart Van Deliveries & Route Geofencing

Ensure your drivers follow their assigned sequence without taking unauthorized detours. Drivers record deliveries with customer digital signatures, geotagged photos, and automatic inventory decrements directly inside the van.

  • Turn-by-turn Beat Sequences: Optimized daily routes saving 15–20% on vehicle diesel.
  • Offline Order Generation: Works seamlessly in basement shops, rural towns, and offline areas.
  • Proof of Delivery (POD): Real-time timestamp, GPS coordinate, and digital customer signature.
Van Dispatch Control
Daily Morning Reconciliation
Drivers verify crate counts via handheld barcode scanner before dispatch. Returns at 5:00 PM are balanced automatically in under 15 minutes.
Credit Exception Engine
Eliminate Price Overrides
When a field rep requests a 3% volume discount or an extended credit limit for an A-tier supermarket, the manager receives a push notification and approves it within 45 seconds.
Module 2 · Commercial Control

Manager Approvals & Credit Controls

Sales reps in the field cannot arbitrarily discount or exceed customer credit lines. All threshold breaches trigger automated approval tickets to the territory sales manager with one-tap WhatsApp and in-app sign-off.

  • Credit Limit Enforcement: Blocks dispatch if an account has unpaid 30-day invoices.
  • Discount Threshold Gates: Multi-tiered authorization based on rep seniority.
  • Complete Audit Log: Every price modification recorded with manager timestamp.
Module 3 · Merchandising

Retail Shelf Space Audits & Share of Shelf

Turn your merchandising reps into frontline intelligence agents. Capture high-resolution photo audits of retail gondolas, calculate facings vs competitor brands, and verify promotional POS displays.

  • Planogram Compliance: Side-by-side comparison of ideal vs actual shelf facings.
  • Competitor Price Tag Capture: Log rival promotions and retail prices in real time.
  • Out-of-Stock (OOS) Alerts: Automated re-order triggers when shelves drop below safety stock.
Shelf Share Analytics
Visual Merchandising Proof
Photos are geotagged and timestamped. Sales directors can audit 100 retail outlets in Westlands or Industrial Area from their central dashboard.
Cashless FMCG
Zero Cash Handling in Vans
The driver selects the order, triggers M-Pesa STK push to the shopkeeper's phone, and the receipt prints via Bluetooth printer in seconds.
Module 4 · Revenue Security

Cashless M-Pesa Collections & Instant Balancing

Eliminate van robbery risk and cash leakage. Enable your drivers and sales reps to collect payments via integrated M-Pesa STK push, Till payments, or bank wire confirmations with instant ERP reconciliation.

  • Automated STK Push: Prompts the merchant immediately for PIN authorization.
  • Instant ERP Reconciliation: Integrates with SAP, QuickBooks, and Microsoft Dynamics.
  • Daily Z-Report Balancing: End-of-day van accounting settled in 15 minutes.
